Offline snapshot

Local loopback benchmark — not production

No demonstrated speedup. Successful-request p95 was nearly unchanged in the steady run and slightly higher in the flap run. Failures remained frequent during flapping. These descriptive differences were not shown to be statistically significant.

Before: main b966dbb · After: integrated candidate 86d382a

Each latency percentile is the median of five per-trial percentiles (24 agents, 10 seconds per trial); successes and failures are summed across trials. Failures include client-side rejections, but the benchmark did not record a separate fail-fast count. Recovery is the maximum observed time from proxy restart to the start of a later successful request.
